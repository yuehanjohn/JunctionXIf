How these numbers are built

Every quote above comes from one open formula: crew × hours × rate × day-factor + kilometres × van rate + extras. The same inputs produce the same number for every operator, so nothing here can be jacked up between your search and your booking. Weekend factors and winter-handling fees are quoted as line items, never buried. Operators pay a listing fee — you never pay a cent.
