The Challenge
This is not simply a “build another insurance app” challenge. The difficult part is identifying a real human need where an insurance company can provide meaningful value without making insurance itself the centre of the experience. The best idea might prevent something from going wrong, make a life change easier, help someone make a better decision, connect useful services together, or create something completely different. AI, data and automation can absolutely be part of the solution, but technology is not the goal. The goal is something people would genuinely value — with a believable reason why it would also make sense for If as a business.

Resources
Teams are encouraged to use public and open data that can help bring their concept to life. Possible resources include: • Publicly available insurance terms and conditions from If and other insurance companies. • Open data sources such as weather and forecasting data. • Relevant public APIs, statistics and datasets. • Other external data sources identified by the team. Customer-specific or confidential insurance data will not be required for the challenge.
